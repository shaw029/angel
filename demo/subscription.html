Common questions

What happens when my trial ends?
Your workspace is locked immediately — you won't be able to access notes, projects, or exports. Upgrading before the trial ends preserves your trial pricing, which is lower than our standard rate.
Can I cancel the annual plan?
You can cancel at any time to stop renewal. Annual plans are non-refundable for the current billing period. Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days notice.
Is the discount for the first year only?
The 40% annual discount applies as long as you stay subscribed. If you cancel and re-subscribe later, standard pricing applies — you won't be able to reclaim the introductory rate.
Can I switch from monthly to annual later?
Yes, but you'll pay the standard annual rate at that time. Upgrading now while you're in your trial locks in the promotional rate.
